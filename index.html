Seongwoong Shim

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Decision Making Lab at Korea University, advised by Prof. Byung-Jun Lee. I push the boundaries of intelligent systems by leveraging reinforcement learning to enhance both the performance and the efficiency of modern foundation models, across language, vision–language, and diffusion models.

I am equally interested in the reverse direction: using large pretrained priors to make offline decision making and skill discovery work on real, task-agnostic data.
